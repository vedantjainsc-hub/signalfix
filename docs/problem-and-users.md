# Problem and Users

## Problem statement

Customer-operations teams can read and aggregate complaints, but the difficult work begins after a theme appears: determine whether it is credible, connect it to an operating process, compare interventions, approve a bounded pilot, and preserve why the decision was made.

SignalFix turns that fragmented sequence into one evidence-backed workflow.

## Primary user

**Customer Operations Director**

Weekly job to be done:

> Tell me which customer-friction problem deserves investigation, show the evidence and uncertainty, and help me approve a measurable pilot.

## Secondary users

- **Customer Experience / Compliance Analyst:** reviews themes, evidence, and exceptions.
- **COO / Head of Consumer Banking:** reviews the remediation portfolio, owners, impact assumptions, and outcomes.

## Fictional demo organization

Northstar Bank is a fictional consumer bank used only to demonstrate internal service data and economics. Public CFPB complaints are external market signals and are never presented as Northstar customer complaints.

## Core product questions

1. What changed?
2. Why should I believe it?
3. Does it appear internally?
4. What could we do?
5. What are we approving?
6. What did we decide?
7. Did the pilot work?

## Non-goals

- Ranking real companies by raw complaint volume.
- Predicting legal or regulatory violations.
- Making credit, eligibility, or individual customer decisions.
- Treating a generated causal explanation as a proven root cause.
- Automating production-wide remediation without human approval.
