# Complaint Taxonomy v0.1

The taxonomy is intentionally small enough for a new reviewer to understand.

## Process stage

- acquisition
- account_setup
- payments
- billing
- purchase_disputes
- fraud_security
- rewards
- servicing
- collections
- account_closure
- unknown

## Failure mode

- unclear_communication
- unauthorized_action
- delayed_resolution
- incorrect_fee_or_charge
- system_or_process_error
- access_blocked
- policy_dispute
- handoff_failure
- suspected_fraud
- other
- unknown

## Harm type

- financial_loss
- access_loss
- time_burden
- credit_or_reputation_risk
- privacy_or_security_concern
- emotional_distress
- unclear

## Urgency rubric

- **low:** inconvenience with no stated financial, access, credit, or security harm.
- **medium:** repeated contact, material delay, or limited financial/time burden.
- **high:** substantial financial/access impact, unresolved fraud, or credible credit harm.
- **critical:** immediate ongoing security risk or severe access/financial harm requiring human escalation.

Urgency is decision support, not a legal or regulatory conclusion.
